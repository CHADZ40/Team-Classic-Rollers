## About the site

Team Classic Rollers is where the team trains, competes and keeps score. The site puts all of that in one place. Fans can follow the numbers, new players can learn the game, and members can get their scores on the board from their phone at the lanes.

## What's on it

| Page | What you'll find |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | A 3D bowling hero that rolls for a strike as you scroll, the live scoreboard, the three squads, the team's leaders, and the latest news |
| **Team** | The full roster, with search, squad filters and sorting |
| **Bowler profiles** | Each bowler's average, progress towards their goal, achievements and score history |
| **Rankings** | Live standings by average, games and total pins, for the whole team or a single squad |
| **Learn** | Bowling made simple: how scoring works, a tap-to-score calculator, first-game tips and a glossary |
| **About** | The story and values behind the team |
| **Submit a score** | Send in games and pins, with a photo of the lane screen as proof |
| **Member sign-in** | Members sign in with Telegram to see their stats, profile, notifications and training |

## The squads

- **Team A** is the experienced core. They set the standard in practice and carry it into competition.
- **Team B** is focused and climbing, all about growth, sharper spare shooting and tougher competition.
- **Team C** is the next generation, building the habits and confidence that win games later.

## Run by the team

Everything on the site is managed from a private admin dashboard, with no code involved.

- **Scores:** review submitted scores and approve or reject them, on the dashboard or straight from Telegram
- **Players:** add bowlers, update photos and bios, and correct stats
- **Leaders:** add, edit, remove and reorder the people introduced on the home page, each with a role, picture and caption
- **News:** publish announcements, tournaments and results
- **Website text:** change the wording on the Home and About pages
- **Member accounts:** link members to their Telegram accounts
- **Activity:** see a record of every change

## Built with

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Website and admin** | React, React Router, Vite |
| **3D and motion** | Three.js, React Three Fiber, Motion, Lenis |
| **Backend** | Node.js, Express |
| **Member sign-in and score alerts** | Telegram bot |

The site works on phones as well as it does on desktop, because that's what people have in their hands at the lanes. It also respects each visitor's reduced-motion setting.
